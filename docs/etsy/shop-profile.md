### Historia

```
Gradientwall started with a simple idea: the screen you look at most should feel good to look at.

Each pack is a small, curated collection built around one visual idea: thick acrylic brushstrokes in Impasto, the circles and flat colour fields of the Bauhaus school in Dessau. Every design ships in phone, 4K and 5K sizes, so your whole setup matches.

How the packs are made: the artwork is created with AI-assisted image generation, then selected, upscaled, cropped and colour-checked by hand. Only a fraction of what gets generated makes it into a pack, and anything that doesn't read well behind your clock and icons is left out.

Based in Tarragona, Spain. Questions or a size you need? Send a message. I usually reply within a day.
```
